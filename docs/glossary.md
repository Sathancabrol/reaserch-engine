# Research Engine Glossary v0.1

## ResearchQuestion
Question submitted to the engine and normalized into a researchable representation.

## ProblemRepresentation
Structured interpretation of the question: intent, scope, assumptions, ambiguity and expected answer.

## SubQuestion
Independent question generated from the main question to make investigation tractable.

## ResearchPlan
Explicit plan linking questions, evidence requirements, searches, methods, analyses and stopping criteria.

## Source
A document, dataset, webpage, study, database or other information origin used by the investigation.

## Evidence
A specific information unit relevant to evaluating a claim.

## Claim
Atomic proposition that can independently be supported, contradicted or left unresolved.

## Hypothesis
Provisional proposition or explanation that can generate predictions or guide investigation.

## Finding
Result produced by analysis of evidence.

## Synthesis
Structured integration of multiple findings into an answer or model.

## Contradiction
Relationship between claims, findings or sources that appear inconsistent and require explanation.

## Uncertainty
Residual lack of knowledge or precision surrounding a claim, finding or conclusion.

## Provenance
Information describing where an artifact came from, how it was transformed and how it was used.

## Source Quality
Assessment of characteristics of a source such as relevance, authority, rigor, transparency, recency and independence.

## Evidence Strength
Assessment of how strongly a particular piece or body of evidence bears on a claim.

## Claim Confidence
Calibrated assessment of how strongly the total available evidence supports a claim.

## Exploratory Analysis
Analysis intended to discover patterns or generate hypotheses.

## Confirmatory Analysis
Analysis designed to test a previously specified hypothesis or prediction.

## Information Gain
Expected reduction in relevant uncertainty produced by a research action.

## Sufficiency
The state in which additional available research is unlikely to materially improve the answer relative to its cost or budget.

## Research Run
Complete auditable execution of the engine for one question, including iterations and final stopping decision.

## Iteration
One cycle of planning, evidence acquisition, evaluation, analysis and critique within a Research Run.

## Quality Gate
A checkpoint that must be satisfied before the investigation advances or finalizes.

## Human Review
Explicit escalation when the engine cannot responsibly resolve uncertainty, ambiguity, risk or conflict autonomously.
