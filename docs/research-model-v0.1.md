# Canonical Research Model v0.1

The engine uses an epistemically explicit chain:

```text
Question → SubQuestion → Hypothesis
                         ↓
Source → Evidence → Claim → Finding → Inference → Conclusion
                    ↘ Contradiction ↗
```

## Rules

1. A source is not evidence merely because it exists.
2. Evidence must retain provenance, locator and relevant context.
3. Claims can have supporting and counter-evidence.
4. Contradictions are objects to investigate, not errors to hide.
5. Findings summarize assessed claims; inferences expose assumptions and alternatives.
6. Conclusions must remain traceable to findings/inferences and unresolved limitations.
7. Confidence is not truth and must not replace provenance or uncertainty.
8. Exploratory research may generate hypotheses without treating them as confirmed claims.
9. Confirmatory research should preserve the pre-specified prediction and analysis intent.
10. The final dossier must be reconstructible from the research state and graph.

## Context dimensions

Where relevant, evidence carries population, setting, temporal window, task and measurement conditions. This prevents decontextualized generalization.

## Graph mapping

- Knowledge graph: concepts, theories, models and literature.
- Evidence graph: sources, evidence and claims.
- Inference graph: assumptions, alternatives, findings and conclusions.

These are complementary representations, not interchangeable truth stores.
